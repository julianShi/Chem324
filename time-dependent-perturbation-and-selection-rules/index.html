Time-Dependent Perturbation Theory and Selection Rules

What you will learn

  • Transitions between states: When a system is exposed to a time-dependent perturbation, such as an oscillating electromagnetic field, it can make transitions between the stationary states of the unperturbed Hamiltonian.

  • First-order transition amplitude: Expanding the time-dependent state in the unperturbed eigenbasis and keeping the leading term gives a simple integral for the amplitude of going from an initial state ∣i⟩|i\rangle∣i⟩ to a final state ∣f⟩|f\rangle∣f⟩.

  • Resonance and energy conservation: For an oscillating field, the transition is large only when the photon energy matches the level spacing, Ef−Ei=±ℏωE_f - E_i = \pm\hbar\omegaEf​−Ei​=±ℏω.

  • Selection rules: The transition probability is proportional to ∣μfi∣2|\mu_{fi}|^2∣μfi​∣2. When this dipole matrix element vanishes by symmetry, the transition is forbidden. For hydrogen-like atoms this yields Δl=±1\Delta l = \pm 1Δl=±1 and Δml=0,±1\Delta m_l = 0, \pm 1Δml​=0,±1.

中文翻译

  • 各态间的跃迁：当系统受到时依扰动，如振荡电磁场时，可使无摄动哈密顿量的驻态之间发生跃迁。

  • 一阶跃迁幅度: 将时依状态展开到无扰动本征基并保留首项，可得到从初态 ∣i⟩|i\rangle∣i⟩ 到末态 ∣f⟩|f\rangle∣f⟩ 跃迁幅度的简单积分。

  • 共振与能量守恒：对于摆动场，仅当光子能量与能级间距匹配时跃迁才显著，Ef−Ei=±ℏωE_f - E_i = \pm\hbar\omegaEf​−Ei​=±ℏω。

  • 选取规则： 过渡概率与 ∣μfi∣2|\mu_{fi}|^2∣μfi​∣2 成正比。当此偶极矩阵元因对称性而消去时，该跃迁被禁止。对于氢样子原子，这产生了 Δl=±1\Delta l = \pm 1Δl=±1 和 Δml=0,±1\Delta m_l = 0, \pm 1Δml​=0,±1。

Setup: stationary states and a time-dependent perturbation¶

We consider a Hamiltonian split into an exactly solvable part and a weak, time-dependent perturbation:

中文翻译

我们考虑一个哈密顿量，将其分为一个可精确求解的部分和一个微弱的、时变的微扰：

H^(t)=H^0+V^(t)\hat{H}(t) = \hat{H}_0 + \hat{V}(t)H^(t)=H^0​+V^(t)
(1)

The unperturbed Hamiltonian H^0\hat{H}_0H^0​ has known eigenstates and eigenvalues:

中文翻译

未受微扰的哈密顿量 H^0\hat{H}_0H^0​ 具有已知的本征态和本征值：

H^0∣n⟩=En∣n⟩\hat{H}_0 |n\rangle = E_n |n\rangleH^0​∣n⟩=En​∣n⟩
(2)

We expand the full time-dependent state in the stationary basis:

中文翻译

我们将完整的时间依赖态在定态基底中展开：

∣Ψ(t)⟩=∑ncn(t) e−iEnt/ℏ∣n⟩|\Psi(t)\rangle = \sum_n c_n(t)\, e^{-iE_n t/\hbar} |n\rangle∣Ψ(t)⟩=n∑​cn​(t)e−iEn​t/ℏ∣n⟩
(3)

The time-dependent coefficients cn(t)c_n(t)cn​(t) encode the transitions between stationary states caused by V^(t)\hat{V}(t)V^(t).

中文翻译

时变系数 cn(t)c_n(t)cn​(t) 编码了由 V^(t)\hat{V}(t)V^(t) 引起的定态间跃迁。

Deriving the first-order transition amplitude¶

Insert the expansion into the time-dependent Schrodinger equation:

中文翻译

将展开式代入含时薛定谔方程：

iℏ∂∂t∣Ψ(t)⟩=H^(t)∣Ψ(t)⟩i\hbar \frac{\partial}{\partial t}|\Psi(t)\rangle = \hat{H}(t)|\Psi(t)\rangleiℏ∂t∂​∣Ψ(t)⟩=H^(t)∣Ψ(t)⟩
(4)

After some algebra, using orthonormality ⟨m∣n⟩=δmn\langle m|n\rangle = \delta_{mn}⟨m∣n⟩=δmn​, we obtain the equation of motion for the coefficients:

中文翻译

经过一些代数运算，利用正交归一性 ⟨m∣n⟩=δmn\langle m|n\rangle = \delta_{mn}⟨m∣n⟩=δmn​，我们得到系数的运动方程：

iℏ c˙m(t)=∑ncn(t) Vmn(t) eiωmnti\hbar\, \dot{c}_m(t) = \sum_n c_n(t)\, V_{mn}(t)\, e^{i\omega_{mn} t}iℏc˙m​(t)=n∑​cn​(t)Vmn​(t)eiωmn​t
(5)

where

Vmn(t)=⟨m∣V^(t)∣n⟩,ωmn=Em−EnℏV_{mn}(t) = \langle m|\hat V(t)|n\rangle, \qquad \omega_{mn} = \frac{E_m - E_n}{\hbar}Vmn​(t)=⟨m∣V^(t)∣n⟩,ωmn​=ℏEm​−En​​
(6)

Assume the system starts in state ∣i⟩|i\rangle∣i⟩ at t=0t=0t=0:

中文翻译

假设系统在 t=0t=0t=0 时处于态 ∣i⟩|i\rangle∣i⟩：

cn(0)=δni,ci(0)=1,cf≠i(0)=0c_n(0) = \delta_{ni}, \quad c_i(0) = 1, \quad c_{f\neq i}(0) = 0cn​(0)=δni​,ci​(0)=1,cf=i​(0)=0
(7)

In first-order perturbation theory we approximate cn(t)≈δnic_n(t) \approx \delta_{ni}cn​(t)≈δni​ on the right-hand side (the perturbation is weak, so the population stays mostly in the initial state). The equation for cf(t)c_f(t)cf​(t) becomes

中文翻译

在一阶摄动理论中，我们近似 cn(t)≈δnic_n(t) \approx \delta_{ni}cn​(t)≈δni​ 在右边（摄动弱，所以电子主要留在初态）。 cf(t)c_f(t)cf​(t) 的方程变成

c˙f(1)(t)=−iℏVfi(t) eiωfit\dot{c}_f^{(1)}(t) = -\frac{i}{\hbar} V_{fi}(t)\, e^{i\omega_{fi} t}c˙f(1)​(t)=−ℏi​Vfi​(t)eiωfi​t
(8)

Integrating from 0 to ttt:

cf(1)(t)=−iℏ∫0tVfi(t′) eiωfit′dt′c_f^{(1)}(t) = -\frac{i}{\hbar} \int_0^t V_{fi}(t')\, e^{i\omega_{fi} t'} dt'cf(1)​(t)=−ℏi​∫0t​Vfi​(t′)eiωfi​t′dt′
(9)

This is the first-order transition amplitude from state ∣i⟩|i\rangle∣i⟩ to ∣f⟩|f\rangle∣f⟩.

中文翻译

这是从态 ∣i⟩|i\rangle∣i⟩ 到 ∣f⟩|f\rangle∣f⟩ 的一阶跃迁振幅。

Coupling to light: the dipole approximation¶

For an atom or molecule in a classical electromagnetic field, the electric dipole approximation gives the perturbation

中文翻译

对于处于经典电磁场中的原子或分子，电偶极近似给出了微扰

V^(t)=−μ^⋅E(t)\hat{V}(t) = -\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}\cdot \mathbf{E}(t)V^(t)=−μ^​⋅E(t)
(10)

For a monochromatic, linearly polarized field

E(t)=E0cos⁡(ωt)\mathbf{E}(t) = \mathbf{E}_0 \cos(\omega t)E(t)=E0​cos(ωt)
(11)

the matrix element is

Vfi(t)=⟨f∣V^(t)∣i⟩=−⟨f∣μ^⋅E0∣i⟩cos⁡(ωt)V_{fi}(t) = \langle f|\hat{V}(t)|i\rangle = -\langle f|\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}\cdot \mathbf{E}_0|i\rangle \cos(\omega t)Vfi​(t)=⟨f∣V^(t)∣i⟩=−⟨f∣μ^​⋅E0​∣i⟩cos(ωt)
(12)

Define the (possibly complex) dipole matrix element

μfi=⟨f∣μ^⋅e^∣i⟩\mu_{fi} = \langle f|\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}\cdot \hat{\mathbf{e}}|i\rangleμfi​=⟨f∣μ^​⋅e^∣i⟩
(13)

where e^\hat{\mathbf{e}}e^ is the polarization direction of the field, and let E0=∣E0∣E_0 = |\mathbf{E}_0|E0​=∣E0​∣. Then

中文翻译

其中 e^\hat{\mathbf{e}}e^ 是场的偏振方向，令 E0=∣E0∣E_0 = |\mathbf{E}_0|E0​=∣E0​∣。然后

Vfi(t)=−μfiE0cos⁡(ωt)V_{fi}(t) = -\mu_{fi} E_0 \cos(\omega t)Vfi​(t)=−μfi​E0​cos(ωt)
(14)

Plugging into the transition amplitude:

cf(1)(t)=iE0ℏμfi∫0tcos⁡(ωt′) eiωfit′dt′c_f^{(1)}(t) = \frac{i E_0}{\hbar} \mu_{fi} \int_0^t \cos(\omega t')\, e^{i\omega_{fi} t'} dt'cf(1)​(t)=ℏiE0​​μfi​∫0t​cos(ωt′)eiωfi​t′dt′
(15)

Resonance condition and energy conservation¶

Use the exponential form of the cosine:

cos⁡(ωt′)=12(eiωt′+e−iωt′)\cos(\omega t') = \frac{1}{2}\left(e^{i\omega t'} + e^{-i\omega t'}\right)cos(ωt′)=21​(eiωt′+e−iωt′)
(16)

Then the integral in cf(1)(t)c_f^{(1)}(t)cf(1)​(t) contains terms of the form

中文翻译

那么 cf(1)(t)c_f^{(1)}(t)cf(1)​(t) 中的积分包含如下形式的项

∫0tei(ωfi±ω)t′dt′\int_0^t e^{i(\omega_{fi} \pm \omega)t'} dt'∫0t​ei(ωfi​±ω)t′dt′
(17)

If ωfi±ω\omega_{fi} \pm \omegaωfi​±ω is large, the exponential oscillates rapidly and the integral averages out to a small value. A large transition amplitude occurs when the exponent is nearly stationary:

中文翻译

如果 ωfi±ω\omega_{fi} \pm \omegaωfi​±ω 很大，指数会快速振荡，积分平均会得到一个较小的值。当指数几乎不变时会发生大跃迁幅度：

ωfi−ω≈0⇒ωfi≈ω\omega_{fi} - \omega \approx 0 \quad \Rightarrow \quad \omega_{fi} \approx \omegaωfi​−ω≈0⇒ωfi​≈ω
(18)

This gives the familiar energy-conservation condition for absorption:

中文翻译

这给出了吸收的熟悉 能量守恒条件：

Ef−Ei=ℏωE_f - E_i = \hbar\omegaEf​−Ei​=ℏω
(19)

Similarly, for stimulated emission one finds Ef−Ei=−ℏωE_f - E_i = -\hbar\omegaEf​−Ei​=−ℏω.

中文翻译

同样地，对于受激辐射可得 Ef−Ei=−ℏωE_f - E_i = -\hbar\omegaEf​−Ei​=−ℏω。

From transition amplitudes to selection rules¶

Selection Rules

The transition probability (to first order) is

Pi→f(t)≈∣cf(1)(t)∣2∝∣μfi∣2P_{i\to f}(t) \approx |c_f^{(1)}(t)|^2 \propto |\mu_{fi}|^2Pi→f​(t)≈∣cf(1)​(t)∣2∝∣μfi​∣2
(20)

  • A transition ∣i⟩→∣f⟩|i\rangle \to |f\rangle∣i⟩→∣f⟩ is electric-dipole allowed only if the dipole matrix element is nonzero:

中文翻译

  • 只有当偶极矩阵元非零时，跃迁 ∣i⟩→∣f⟩|i\rangle \to |f\rangle∣i⟩→∣f⟩ 才是电偶极允许的：

μfi=⟨f∣μ^⋅e^∣i⟩≠0\mu_{fi} = \langle f|\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}\cdot \hat{\mathbf{e}}|i\rangle \neq 0μfi​=⟨f∣μ^​⋅e^∣i⟩=0
(21)

If the matrix element is exactly zero due to symmetry, the transition is dipole-forbidden (in first order).

中文翻译

如果矩阵元因对称性而恰好为零，则该跃迁是偶极子禁戒的（一级近似下）。

Selection rules therefore come from:

  • the symmetry properties (angular, parity, and so on) of the wavefunctions ∣i⟩|i\rangle∣i⟩ and ∣f⟩|f\rangle∣f⟩

  • the transformation properties of the operator μ^∼r\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}} \sim \mathbf{r}μ^​∼r (a vector operator)

中文翻译

  • 波函数 ∣i⟩|i\rangle∣i⟩ 和 ∣f⟩|f\rangle∣f⟩ 的对称性质（角、宇称等）

  • 算符 μ^∼r\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}} \sim \mathbf{r}μ^​∼r 的变换性质（矢量算符）

Electric dipole selection rules for hydrogen-like orbitals¶

For an electron in a central potential (for example, a hydrogenic atom), the stationary states are labeled by ∣n,l,ml⟩|n, l, m_l\rangle∣n,l,ml​⟩.

中文翻译

对于中心势场中的电子（例如，类氢原子），定态用 ∣n,l,ml⟩|n, l, m_l\rangle∣n,l,ml​⟩ 标记。

The position operator r\mathbf{r}r transforms like an angular-momentum-1 object (similar to the spherical harmonics Y1mY_{1m}Y1m​). From angular-momentum coupling rules one obtains:

中文翻译

位置算符 r\mathbf{r}r 像角动量-1 物体一样转换（类似球谐函数 Y1mY_{1m}Y1m​）。从角动量耦合规则得出：

Orbital angular momentum selection rule

Δl=lf−li=±1\Delta l = l_f - l_i = \pm 1Δl=lf​−li​=±1
(22)

Magnetic quantum number selection rule

Δml=ml,f−ml,i=0,±1\Delta m_l = m_{l,f} - m_{l,i} = 0, \pm 1Δml​=ml,f​−ml,i​=0,±1
(23)

  • Start from the time-dependent Schrodinger equation with H^(t)=H^0+V^(t)\hat{H}(t) = \hat{H}_0 + \hat{V}(t)H^(t)=H^0​+V^(t).

  • Expand the state in the eigenbasis of H^0\hat{H}_0H^0​ to obtain equations for cn(t)c_n(t)cn​(t).

  • First-order perturbation theory gives a transition amplitude

中文翻译

  • 从含时薛定谔方程出发，其中 H^(t)=H^0+V^(t)\hat{H}(t) = \hat{H}_0 + \hat{V}(t)H^(t)=H^0​+V^(t)。

  • 将态在 H^0\hat{H}_0H^0​ 的本征基下展开，以得到 cn(t)c_n(t)cn​(t) 的方程。

  • 一阶摄动理论给出一个跃迁幅度

cf(1)(t)=−iℏ∫0t⟨f∣V^(t′)∣i⟩ eiωfit′dt′c_f^{(1)}(t) = -\frac{i}{\hbar} \int_0^t \langle f|\hat{V}(t')|i\rangle\, e^{i\omega_{fi} t'} dt'cf(1)​(t)=−ℏi​∫0t​⟨f∣V^(t′)∣i⟩eiωfi​t′dt′
(24)

  • For an oscillating field, the integral is large only when Ef−Ei=±ℏωE_f - E_i = \pm \hbar\omegaEf​−Ei​=±ℏω (energy conservation).

  • The transition probability is proportional to ∣⟨f∣μ^∣i⟩∣2|\langle f|\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}|i\rangle|^2∣⟨f∣μ^​∣i⟩∣2.

  • If the dipole matrix element is zero by symmetry, the transition is forbidden.

  • For electric-dipole transitions in atoms: Δl=±1\Delta l = \pm 1Δl=±1, Δml=0,±1\Delta m_l = 0, \pm 1Δml​=0,±1, and the parity changes.

中文翻译

  • 对于振荡场，仅当 Ef−Ei=±ℏωE_f - E_i = \pm \hbar\omegaEf​−Ei​=±ℏω（能量守恒）时积分才较大。

  • 跃迁概率与 ∣⟨f∣μ^∣i⟩∣2|\langle f|\hat{\boldsymbol{\mu}}|i\rangle|^2∣⟨f∣μ^​∣i⟩∣2 成正比。

  • 如果偶极矩阵元因对称性而为零，则该跃迁是禁戒的。
