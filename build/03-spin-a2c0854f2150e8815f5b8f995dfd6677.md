# Electron Spin

:::{note} **What you will learn**

- **Magnetism** arises from the circular motion of charged particles.
- Atoms with electrons that have nonzero orbital angular momentum generate magnetic moments, making them susceptible to the effects of an external magnetic field.
- The **Stern-Gerlach experiment** established the existence of an intrinsic magnetic moment in electrons, called **spin**, which is affected by a magnetic field independently of orbital angular momentum.
- Despite the name, **spin** is an intrinsic magnetic property of subatomic particles and does not involve literal "spinning" or motion. Spin is a fundamental property of particles, akin to mass or charge.
- Particles with half-integer spins (e.g., $1/2$, $3/2$, $5/2$) are classified as **fermions**, while those with integer spins (e.g., $0$, $1$, $2$) are classified as **bosons**. These two families follow different rules. Fermions obey the **Pauli exclusion principle**, which states that no two identical fermions can occupy the same quantum state simultaneously. In contrast, bosons are not restricted in this way and can occupy the same state, allowing them to "bunch together."
- **Normal Zeeman effect:** the splitting of singlet states (spin zero) in a magnetic field due to the electron's orbital angular momentum, which can be explained classically.
- **Anomalous Zeeman effect:** a more general case where both electron spin and orbital angular momentum contribute to the splitting of energy levels in a magnetic field.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- **磁性** 产生于带电粒子的圆周运动。
- 具有非零轨道角动量电子的原子会产生磁矩，使其易受外部磁场的影响。
- **Stern-Gerlach实验**确立了电子内在磁矩的存在，称为**自旋**，该磁矩受磁场影响独立于轨道角动量。
- 尽管名为**自旋**，但它是亚原粒子的内在磁性质，不涉及字面上的"旋转"或运动。自旋是粒子的基本性质，类似于质量或电荷。
- 自转半整数的粒子（例如 $1/2$，$3/2$，$5/2$）被归类为**费米オン**，而自转整数的粒子（例如 $0$，$1$，$2$）被归类为**玻色子**。这两个家族遵循不同的规则。费米オン遵循**保罗不相容原理**，该原理指出，不允许两个相同的费米オン同时占据同一个量子态。相比之下，玻色子不受此限制，可以占据同一个状态，从而允许它们“聚在一起”。
- **正常Zeeman效应:** 自旋为零的单态在磁场中的分裂，由电子的轨道角动量引起，可用经典理论解释。
- **异常Zeeman效应:** 一个更一般的情况，其中电子自旋和轨道角动量都对磁场中能级的分裂做出贡献。
``````
:::

### Magnetic moment of the electron

- According to classical mechanics, a charged particle like an electron rotating around an axis has a magnetic moment given by:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 根据经典力学，一个像电子一样绕轴旋转的带电粒子具有磁矩，由以下公式给出：
``````

$${\vec{\mu} = \gamma_e\vec{L}}$$

- where $\gamma_e$ is the magnetogyric ratio of the electron, expressed via fundamental constants as $-\frac{e}{2m_e}$. We choose the external magnetic field to lie along the $z$-axis, so it is the $z$ component of $\vec{\mu}$ that matters:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 其中 $\gamma_e$ 为电子的磁当量比，可通过基本常数表示为 $-\frac{e}{2m_e}$。我们将外部磁场沿 $z$ 轴取向，因此 $\vec{\mu}$ 的 $z$ 分量至关重要：
``````

$${\mu_z = -\left(\frac{e}{2m_e}\right)L_z = -\left(\frac{e\hbar}{2m_e}\right)m \equiv -\mu_B m}$$

- where $\mu_B$ is the Bohr magneton. The interaction between a magnetic moment and an external magnetic field is given by the classical expression:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 其中 $\mu_B$ 是玻尔磁子。磁矩与外磁场之间的相互作用由经典表达式给出：
``````

$${U = -\vec{\mu}\cdot\vec{B} = -|\vec{\mu}||\vec{B}|\cos(\alpha)}$$

- where $\alpha$ is the angle between the two vectors. This gives the energy for a bar magnet in the presence of an external field.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 其中 $\alpha$ 是两个向量之间的夹角。这给出了条形磁铁在外部场中存在时的能量。
``````

:::{figure} ./images/mag-orient.jpeg
:alt: Torque on a magnet in an external field
:width: 30%

Fig.3 An external magnetic field exerts a torque on a magnet. The interaction energy is proportional to the field strength $B$ and the magnetic moment $\mu$.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

图 3 外部磁场对磁铁施加力矩。相互作用能与磁场强度 $B$ 和磁矩 $\mu$ 成正比。
``````
:::

- Unlike in quantum mechanics, in classical mechanics any orientation is allowed. When the external field is oriented along the $z$-axis, the classical potential energy of interaction with the field is:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 与量子力学不同，经典力学中任何取向都是允许的。当外场沿 $z$ 轴方向时，与场的相互作用经典势能为：
``````

$${U = -\mu_z B = \frac{eB}{2m_e}L_z}$$

- In quantum mechanics, to get the Hamiltonian term for interaction with the magnetic field, we simply replace the classical angular momentum with the corresponding operator:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 在量子力学中，求与磁场相互作用的哈密顿项，只需将经典角动量替换为对应的算子：
``````

$$
\hat{H}_{mag} = \frac{eB}{2m_e}\hat{L}_z
$$

### Effect of a magnetic field on atoms

- In quantum mechanics, a magnetic moment (here corresponding to a non-$s$ orbital electron) may only take specific orientations.

- The $z$-axis is often called the quantization axis. The eigenvalues of $\hat{L}_z$ give the possible orientations of the magnetic moment with respect to the external field.
- For example, consider an electron in a $2p$ orbital of a hydrogenlike atom. The electron may reside in any of $2p_{+1}$, $2p_0$, or $2p_{-1}$ (degenerate without the field). For these orbitals $L_z$ may take the values $+\hbar$, $0$, $-\hbar$:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 在量子力学中，磁矩（此处对应非 $s$ 轨道电子）只能取特定的取向。
- $z$ 轴常被称为量子化轴。$\hat{L}_z$ 的本征值给出了磁矩相对于外场的可能取向。
- 例如，考虑氢原子样原子中 $2p$ 态电子的情况。电子可以居住在 $2p_{+1}$、$2p_0$ 或 $2p_{-1}$（无场时退化）中。对于这些轨道，$L_z$ 可以取值 $+\hbar$、$0$、$-\hbar$：
``````

$${\hat{L}_z|p_{+1}\rangle = +1\times\hbar|p_{+1}\rangle}$$
$${\hat{L}_z|p_0\rangle = 0\times\hbar|p_{0}\rangle}$$
$${\hat{L}_z|p_{-1}\rangle = -1\times\hbar|p_{-1}\rangle}$$

### A magnetic field modifies the Hamiltonian

- The total quantum mechanical Hamiltonian for a hydrogenlike atom in a magnetic field can be written as:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 氢类原子在磁场中的总量子力学哈密顿量可以写为：
``````

$${\hat{H} = \hat{H}_0 + \frac{eB}{2m_e}\hat{L}_z}$$

- where $\hat{H}_0$ denotes the Hamiltonian in the absence of the magnetic field.
- Since the projection of angular momentum commutes with the Hamiltonian, $[\hat{L}_z, \hat{H}_0] = 0$, both operators share the same eigenfunctions:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 其中 $\hat{H}_0$ 表示无磁场时的哈密顿量。
- 由于角动量的投影与哈密顿量对易，$[\hat{L}_z, \hat{H}_0] = 0$，这两个算符共享相同的本征函数：
``````

$$\hat{L}_z \mid n,l, m_l\rangle = \hbar m_l \mid n,l, m_l\rangle$$

$$\hat{H}_0 \mid n,l, m_l\rangle = E_n \mid n,l, m_l\rangle$$

:::{important} **H-atom in a magnetic field**

$${E_{nlm} = E_n + \mu_B m_l B}$$

- $n = 1, 2, ...$ determines the energy levels $E_n$ in the absence of a magnetic field.
- $\mu_B = \frac{\hbar e}{2 m_e}$ is the constant we call the Bohr magneton.
- $m_l = -l, ..., 0, ..., +l$.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- $n = 1, 2, ...$ 在无磁场情况下决定能级 $E_n$。
- $\mu_B = \frac{\hbar e}{2 m_e}$ 是我们称为玻尔磁子的常数。
- $m_l = -l, ..., 0, ..., +l$.
``````
:::

### Zeeman effect

- We see that the magnetic field now enters the expression for the energy levels.
- The field splits the $(2l + 1)$ degenerate levels. This is called the orbital **Zeeman effect.**
- For instance, the $2p$ level of the hydrogen atom splits into 3 levels at energies $-\mu_B B$, $0$, $+\mu_B B$.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 我们看到磁场现在出现在能级的表达式中。
- 该场将 $(2l + 1)$ 重能级分裂。这称为轨道**塞曼效应**。
- 例如，氢原子的 $2p$ 能级在 $-\mu_B B$、$0$、$+\mu_B B$ 能量处分裂为 3 个能级。
``````

``````{admonition} 中文翻译
图5 Zeeman 效应：当施加外磁场时，氢原子的尖锐光谱线会分裂成多个 closely spaced（接近但未给出具体中文词的原文，此处保留原文或根据语境填充，此处为接近地译为“多个紧密分布的线”）的线。所示是退化的 $2p$ 能级分裂为三个态，其 $m_l = -1, 0, +1$ 有所不同。
``````

### Spin as a tiny magnet

- We come to view the **spin** of subatomic particles in the same way as mass and charge. **Spin is an intrinsic property of subatomic particles like protons, electrons, and photons.**
- Spin manifests itself via a permanent magnetic moment. A particle with spin can interact with magnetic fields just as a charged particle interacts with electric fields. This is why spin is pictorially depicted as a tiny magnet.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 我们将亚原粒子的 **自旋** 看作质量和电荷一样。 **自旋是自旋-1/2粒子（如质子、电子和光子）的内在属性。**
- 自转通过永久磁矩表现出来。带有自转的粒子可以与磁场相互作用，就像带电粒子与电场相互作用一样。这就是为什么自转在 pictorially（图示化地）被描绘成一个微小的磁铁。
``````

:::{figure} ./images/Spin1.jpg
:alt: Spin pictured as a tiny magnet
:width: 60%

Fig.6 Spin is pictured as a tiny permanent magnet associated with each particle.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

图 6 自旋被描绘为与每个粒子相关的微小永久磁铁。
``````
:::

- **The Schrödinger equation does not naturally account for electron spin.** This is because spin lacks a classical analogue, so our approach of taking the classical Hamiltonian and replacing terms with operators does not work directly for spin.
- The concept of electron spin originates from the **Dirac equation**, which is essentially a generalization of the Schrödinger equation to include relativistic effects.
- Fortunately, spin can be incorporated into the Schrödinger equation once we recognize its existence. We simply add an additional quantum number, $s$, which behaves similarly to the orbital angular momentum $l$.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- **薛定谔方程自然不考虑电子自旋。** 这是因为自旋没有经典类比，所以我们将经典哈密顿量中的项替换为算符的方法对自旋直接行不通。
- 电子自旋的概念源于**狄拉克方程**，它本质上是将薛定谔方程推广到包括相对论效应在内。
- 幸运的是，一旦我们认识到自旋的存在，就可以将其纳入薛定谔方程中。我们只需添加一个额外的量子数 $s$，其行为类似于轨道角动量 $l$。
``````

### Quantum numbers and eigenfunctions of spin

- Spin and its projection, $S$ and $S_z$, have the same properties as the angular momentum $L$ and $L_z$. Quantum mechanics requires the number of allowed spin projections to be:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 自转及其各向分量 $S$ 和 $S_z$ 与角动量 $L$ 和 $L_z$ 有相同的性质。量子力学要求允许的自转各向分量数量为：
``````

$$
2s + 1
$$

- Experiments show that we **only observe two values of spin**:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 实验表明我们**只观测到两个自旋值**：
``````

$$
2s + 1 = 2 \quad \Rightarrow \quad s = \frac{1}{2}
$$

- Projections are determined by the **quantum number $m_s$**, an analog of $m_l$:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 投影由 **量子数 $m_s$** 决定，它是 $m_l$ 的类比：
``````

$$
m_s = -s, +s = -1/2, +1/2
$$

- We expect two eigenfunctions for the two outcomes and denote the eigenfunctions of $S_z$ as $\alpha$ and $\beta$:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 我们预期两个本征函数对应两个结果，并将 $S_z$ 的本征函数记为 $\alpha$ 和 $\beta$：
``````

:::{important} **Eigenfunctions of spin, $\alpha$ and $\beta$**

- **Spin is an intrinsic angular momentum with a fixed eigenvalue:**


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- **自旋是具有固定本征值的内禀角动量：**
``````

$$S = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\hbar$$

$$S_z = \pm \frac{1}{2}\hbar$$

- **Spin eigenfunctions and eigenvalues:**

$$
\hat{S}^2 |\alpha\rangle = \hbar^2 s(s+1) |\alpha\rangle = \frac{3}{4} \hbar^2 |\alpha\rangle
$$

$$
\hat{S}^2 |\beta\rangle = \hbar^2 s(s+1) |\beta\rangle = \frac{3}{4} \hbar^2 |\beta\rangle
$$

$$
\hat{S}_z |\alpha\rangle = +\frac{1}{2} \hbar |\alpha\rangle
$$

$$
\hat{S}_z |\beta\rangle = -\frac{1}{2} \hbar |\beta\rangle
$$
:::

### Orthogonality and normalization of the spin part of the wavefunction

- Note that the following operators commute: $\hat{H}$, $\hat{L}^2$, $\hat{L}_z$, $\hat{S}^2$, and $\hat{S}_z$. This implies that all these quantities can be specified simultaneously.

- The spin eigenfunctions $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are, as expected, orthonormal:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 注意以下算符交换：$\hat{H}$、$\hat{L}^2$、$\hat{L}_z$、$\hat{S}^2$ 和 $\hat{S}_z$。这意味着这些量可以同时指定。
- 自旋本征函数 $\alpha$ 和 $\beta$ 如预期那样是标准正交的：
``````

$$
\int \alpha^* \alpha \, d\sigma \equiv \langle \alpha | \alpha \rangle = \int \beta^* \beta \, d\sigma \equiv \langle \beta | \beta \rangle = 1
$$

$$
\int \alpha^* \beta \, d\sigma \equiv \langle \alpha | \beta \rangle = \int \beta^* \alpha \, d\sigma \equiv \langle \beta | \alpha \rangle = 0
$$

- To completely describe a hydrogenlike atom, the wavefunction must include the spin component. Thus the total wavefunction is a product of the spatial wavefunction and the spin part:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 要完整描述氢样子原子，波函数必须包含自旋部分。因此总波函数是空间波函数和自旋部分的乘积：
``````

$$
|n,l, m_l, m_s\rangle = \psi_{n, l, m_l}(r, \theta, \phi)\,\sigma(s), \quad \text{where } \sigma = \alpha \text{ or } \beta
$$

- Wavefunctions differing in their spin part are orthogonal:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 自旋部分不同的波函数是正交的：
``````

$$\langle 1, 0, 0, +1/2 \mid 1, 0, 0, -1/2\rangle = 0$$

:::{tip} **Action of spin operators on eigenstates**
:class: dropdown

In quantum mechanics the spin of an electron is represented by the spin operator $\vec{S}$, with components $S_x$, $S_y$, and $S_z$. For spin-$\frac{1}{2}$ particles, these operators are represented by the **Pauli matrices** $\sigma_x$, $\sigma_y$, and $\sigma_z$ (multiplied by $\frac{\hbar}{2}$):


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

在量子力学中，电子的自旋由自旋算子 $\vec{S}$ 表示，其分量为 $S_x$、$S_y$ 和 $S_z$。对于自旋-$\frac{1}{2}$ 粒子，这些算子由 **Pauli 矩阵** $\sigma_x$、$\sigma_y$ 和 $\sigma_z$ 表示（乘以 $\frac{\hbar}{2}$）：
``````

$$
S_x = \frac{\hbar}{2} \sigma_x, \quad S_y = \frac{\hbar}{2} \sigma_y, \quad S_z = \frac{\hbar}{2} \sigma_z
$$

The Pauli matrices are:

$$
\sigma_x = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \sigma_y = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -i \\ i & 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \sigma_z = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

The spin eigenstates $\alpha$ and $\beta$ correspond to "spin-up" and "spin-down" along the $z$-axis:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

自旋本征态 $\alpha$ 和 $\beta$ 分别对应 $z$ 轴方向的“自旋向上”和“自旋向下”：
``````

$$
\alpha = \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \beta = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix}
$$

1. **$S_z$ operator:**

$$
S_z \alpha = \frac{\hbar}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{\hbar}{2} \alpha
$$

$$
S_z \beta = \frac{\hbar}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & -1 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = -\frac{\hbar}{2} \beta
$$

2. **$S_x$ operator:**

$$
S_x \alpha = \frac{\hbar}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{\hbar}{2} \beta
$$

$$
S_x \beta = \frac{\hbar}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{\hbar}{2} \alpha
$$

3. **$S_y$ operator:**

$$
S_y \alpha = \frac{\hbar}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -i \\ i & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 1 \\ 0 \end{pmatrix} = \frac{i \hbar}{2} \beta
$$

$$
S_y \beta = \frac{\hbar}{2} \begin{pmatrix} 0 & -i \\ i & 0 \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} = -\frac{i \hbar}{2} \alpha
$$

**Summary table**

| Operator | $\alpha$ (spin-up)            | $\beta$ (spin-down)            |
|----------|-------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| $S_z$    | $\frac{\hbar}{2} \alpha$      | $-\frac{\hbar}{2} \beta$       |
| $S_x$    | $\frac{\hbar}{2} \beta$       | $\frac{\hbar}{2} \alpha$       |
| $S_y$    | $\frac{i \hbar}{2} \beta$     | $-\frac{i \hbar}{2} \alpha$    |
:::

### Magnetic moment due to spin

- Analogously, the $\hat{S}_x$ and $\hat{S}_y$ operators can be defined. These do not commute with $\hat{S}_z$. Because electrons have spin angular momentum, the unpaired electrons in silver atoms (Stern-Gerlach experiment) produce an overall magnetic moment (the "two spots" of silver atoms). The spin magnetic moment is proportional to the spin angular momentum:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 类似地，可以定义 $\hat{S}_x$ 和 $\hat{S}_y$ 算符。这些算符不与 $\hat{S}_z$ 交换。由于电子具有自旋角动量，银原子（斯滕-格拉赫实验）中未成对的电子产生整体磁矩（银原子的“两条斑点”）。自旋磁矩与自旋角动量成正比：
``````

$${\vec{\hat{\mu}}_S = -\frac{g_e e}{2m_e}\vec{\hat{S}}}$$

- where $g_e$ is the free-electron $g$-factor ($2.002322$ from quantum electrodynamics). The $z$-component of the spin magnetic moment is ($z$ is the quantization axis):


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown
``````

### Spin-orbit coupling

- Having two sources of magnetic field in atoms, one due to orbital momentum and another due to spin, there arises the possibility that these microscopic magnets can interact.

- This possibility is indeed realized and is known as **spin-orbit coupling.** A new term is added to the Hamiltonian to account for it:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 由于原子中有两种磁场来源，一种来自轨道动量，另一种来自自旋，因此产生了这类微观磁体相互作用的可能性。
- 这种可能性确实实现了，称为**自旋-轨道耦合**。哈密顿量中增加了一个新项来解释它：
``````

$$\hat{H} = \hat{H}_0 + \frac{A}{r^3} \cdot \hat{L}\cdot\hat{S}$$

- where $A$ is a constant, $\hat{H}_0$ is the Hamiltonian for the H-atom without spin, and $\hat{L}$ and $\hat{S}$ are the orbital and spin angular momentum operators. Notice two things: first, the term decays as $1/r^3$, faster than the potential energy, so this term makes a much smaller contribution than $\hat{H}_0$.

- Second, because of spin-orbit coupling, **neither $\hat{L}$ nor $\hat{S}$ commutes with the Hamiltonian.** Hence one needs a **new quantum number $J$** to specify H-atom states. This new quantum number is the **total angular momentum** $\vec{J} = \vec{L} + \vec{S}$.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 其中 $A$ 为常数，$\hat{H}_0$ 为无自转氢原子的哈密顿量，$\hat{L}$ 和 $\hat{S}$ 为轨道和自转角动量算符。注意两点：首先，该项随 $1/r^3$ 衰减，比势能衰减快，因此该项的贡献远小于 $\hat{H}_0$。
- 其次，由于自旋-轨道耦合，**既不 $\hat{L}$ 也不 $\hat{S}$ 与哈密顿量对易。** 因此需要一个 **新的量子数 $J$** 来指定氢原子态。这个新的量子数是 **总角动量** $\vec{J} = \vec{L} + \vec{S}$。
``````

### Determining total angular momentum values for the H-atom

1. **Understand angular momentum coupling.** In a hydrogen atom, the total angular momentum $\vec{J}$ is the vector sum of the orbital angular momentum $\vec{L}$ and the spin angular momentum $\vec{S}$:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- **理解角动量耦合.** 在氢原子中，总角动量 $\vec{J}$ 是轨道角动量 $\vec{L}$ 和自转角动量 $\vec{S}$ 的矢量和：
``````

$$
\vec{J} = \vec{L} + \vec{S}
$$

Here $l$ is the orbital angular momentum quantum number ($l = 0, 1, 2, \dots$) and $s$ is the spin quantum number ($s = \frac{1}{2}$ for an electron).


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

此处 $l$ 为轨道角动量量子数（$l = 0, 1, 2, \dots$），$s$ 为自旋量子数（电子的 $s = \frac{1}{2}$）。
``````

2. **Determine the possible $j$ values.** The quantum number $j$ represents the magnitude of $\vec{J}$ and takes the values:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- **确定可能的 $j$ 值。** 量子数 $j$ 表示 $\vec{J}$ 的大小，取值为：
``````

$$
j = |l - s|, |l - s| + 1, \dots, (l + s)
$$

3. **Calculate $j$ based on $l$ and $s$.** Since $s = \frac{1}{2}$ for a single electron:

- **If $l = 0$:** $j = \frac{1}{2}$ only.
- **If $l = 1$:** $j = \frac{1}{2}$ and $j = \frac{3}{2}$.
- **If $l = 2$:** $j = \frac{3}{2}$ and $j = \frac{5}{2}$.
- **In general:** $j = l \pm \frac{1}{2}$.

4. **Interpretation.** These $j$ values correspond to the quantized total angular momentum. The magnitude is $|\vec{J}| = \sqrt{j(j+1)}\,\hbar$. The $j$ values determine the energy-level splitting due to spin-orbit coupling (fine structure).


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- **根据 $l$ 和 $s$ 计算 $j$。** 因为单电子的 $s = \frac{1}{2}$：
- **如果 $l = 0$：** $j = \frac{1}{2}$ 仅此一个值。
- **如果 $l = 1$：** $j = \frac{1}{2}$ 和 $j = \frac{3}{2}$。
- **如果 $l = 2$：** $j = \frac{3}{2}$ 和 $j = \frac{5}{2}$。
- **一般情况下:** $j = l \pm \frac{1}{2}$。
- **解释。** 这些 $j$ 值对应于量子化的总角动量。其大小为 $|\vec{J}| = \sqrt{j(j+1)}\,\hbar$。$j$ 值决定了自旋-轨道耦合（精细结构）导致的能级分裂：
``````

### Term symbols

- Because of spin-orbit coupling, the energy levels are no longer described by $l$ and $s$ separately. This is why one introduces term symbols, which encode the total spin multiplicity $(2S+1)$, the angular momentum $L$, and the total angular momentum $J = L + S$. The word "total" takes on more meaning for multi-electron atoms, where angular momenta are summed. For the H-atom, $S = 1/2$ and $L = l$:


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 由于自旋-轨道耦合，能级不再仅由 $l$ 和 $s$ 分别描述。这就是为什么引入了符号，它们编码了总自旋倍性 $(2S+1)$、角动量 $L$ 以及总角动量 $J = L + S$。对于多电子原子，“总”一词有了更深的含义，因为角动量是相加的。对于氢原子，$S = 1/2$ 且 $L = l$：
``````

$$^{2S+1}L_J$$

Determine the microstates of $l = 1$ and $l = 2$ resulting from spin-orbit coupling.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译

确定由自旋-轨道耦合产生的 $l = 1$ 和 $l = 2$ 的微观态。
``````

:::{admonition} **Solution:**
:class: dropdown solution

The $j = l + s$ values follow the rules of spatial quantization of angular momentum, just like $l$ and $s$: the projection of $J$, namely $m_j$, can take the values $l+s, l+s-1, \ldots, -(l+s)$. Since $s = 1/2$, we get $j = l + 1/2$ and $j = l - 1/2$. The total number of microstates equals the 2 values of $m_s$ ($+1/2, -1/2$) times the $2l+1$ values of $m_l$, for a total of $2(2l+1)$ microstates.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

$j = l + s$ 的值遵循角动量的空间量子化规则，就像 $l$ 和 $s$ 一样：$J$ 的投影，即 $m_j$，可以取的值为 $l+s, l+s-1, \ldots, -(l+s)$。因为 $s = 1/2$，我们得到 $j = l + 1/2$ 和 $j = l - 1/2$。微观状态的总数等于 $m_s$ 的 2 个值（$+1/2, -1/2$）乘以 $m_l$ 的 $2l+1$ 个值，总共 $2(2l+1)$ 个微观状态。
``````

For $l = 1$: the maximum projection is $m_j = 1 + 1/2 = 3/2$, coming from $j = 3/2$; the minimum is $m_j = l - 1/2 = 1/2$, coming from $j = 1/2$. We thus have two terms, $^2P_{3/2}$ and $^2P_{1/2}$.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

对于 $l = 1$：最大投影为 $m_j = 1 + 1/2 = 3/2$，来自 $j = 3/2$；最小值为 $m_j = l - 1/2 = 1/2$，来自 $j = 1/2$。因此我们有两个项，$^2P_{3/2}$ 和 $^2P_{1/2}$。
``````

### The "anomalous" Zeeman effect

- While the Zeeman effect in some atoms (e.g., hydrogen) showed the expected equally spaced triplet, in other atoms the magnetic field split the lines into four, six, or even more lines, with some spacings wider than expected. These deviations were labeled the **anomalous Zeeman effect** and were very puzzling to early researchers.
- The explanation gave additional insight into electron spin. With the inclusion of electron spin in the total angular momentum, the other multiplets fall into a consistent picture. So what has historically been called the "anomalous" Zeeman effect is really the normal Zeeman effect once electron spin is included.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 虽然在某些原子（如氢）的塞曼效应中表现出预期的等间距三线，但在其他原子中，磁场将谱线分裂为四线、六线，甚至更多，且某些间距比预期更宽。这些偏差被称为**异常塞曼效应**，对早期研究人员来说非常令人困惑。
- 该解释对电子自旋提供了额外的见解。在总角动量中包含电子自旋后，其他多重态落入了一个一致的图景中。历史上被称为“异常”泽曼效应的其实是正泽曼效应，一旦包含了电子自旋。
``````

### Selection rules for electronic transitions

- Just as for other model systems, the probability of an electronic transition in the H-atom is given by the transition moment (or its $z$-projection), $\langle n', l', m_l', m_s' \mid z \mid n, l, m_l, m_s \rangle$. Evaluating this expression using the properties of the special functions yields the following selection rules.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 正如其他模型系统一样，氢原子中电子跃迁的概率由跃迁矩元（或其 $z$ 分量）给出，$\langle n', l', m_l', m_s' \mid z \mid n, l, m_l, m_s \rangle$。利用特殊函数的性质求该表达式得到以下选取规则。
``````

:::{important} **Selection rules for electronic transitions in the H-atom**

$$\Delta L = \pm 1$$

$$\Delta S = 0$$

$$\Delta J = 0, \pm 1 \quad (0 \rightarrow 0 \text{ forbidden})$$
:::

### Summary of spin and angular momentum

- Spin emerges naturally once one accounts for relativistic effects, as originally shown by Paul Dirac. Except in special cases, relativistic effects are not significant enough to include in quantum mechanics, so we incorporate spin as an additional degree of freedom that has not been accounted for but is known to exist.


``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown

- 自转在考虑相对论效应后自然产生，这最初是由保罗·狄拉克展示的。除非是特殊情况，否则相对论效应在量子力学中不足以显著影响，因此我们将自转视为一个额外的自由度，该自由度尚未被考虑但已知存在。
``````

| *Angular momentum*          | *Spin momentum*           |
| :----------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| $\hat{L} = \hat{r}\times \hat{p}$<br>$\hat{L}_z = -i\hbar \left(x\frac{\partial}{\partial y} - y\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\right)$ | $\hat{S}$<br>$\hat{S}_z$                                     |
| $l = 0, 1, 2, 3, ...$<br>$m_l = -l...0...l$                          | $s = 1/2$ <br> $m_s = -1/2, 1/2$                                  |
| $\mid l, m_l\rangle = Y_{l, m_l}$                               | $\mid s, m_s\rangle = \alpha, \beta$<br>$\mid 1/2, +1/2\rangle = \alpha$ <br>$\mid 1/2, -1/2\rangle = \beta$ |

The spin multiplicity is $2S + 1 = 2$ and the orbital letter for $l = 4$ is $G$. The two term symbols are therefore $^2G_{9/2}$ and $^2G_{7/2}$. The total number of microstates is $2(2l+1) = 2 \times 9 = 18$, distributed as $(2j+1) = 10$ states for $j = 9/2$ and $8$ states for $j = 7/2$.

``````{admonition} 中文翻译
:class: dropdown
``````
